LinkedIn post (draft)

🕵️ I just open-sourced Fraud Ring Investigator: a Java engine + live dashboard that catches UPI mule rings and e-commerce device farms.

The problem: every payment in a ring looks normal. The pattern (shared phone → money loop → ATM cash-out in ~10 seconds) is the giveaway.

What I'm proudest of is not the 100% score. It's what broke along the way: • At low traffic precision was perfect. At 8× traffic it fell to 85%, because 15 ordinary payments can add up to look like a ring. Fixed, tested, documented. • One "false alarm" was really my scorer ignoring the farm I had planted. • A test passed while my API returned the wrong JSON, because the assertion was too loose.

26 tests, zero dependencies, runs with just a JDK. Synthetic data only, and the README says plainly what it can't do.
