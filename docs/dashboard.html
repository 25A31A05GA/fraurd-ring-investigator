How a risk score is built

Each pattern adds points: shared device 35, rapid cash-out 25, circular transfers 25, refund abuse 20 (new account with mostly refunded orders), fan-in 15 (many people paying one account).

Normal behaviour is ignored: families sharing one phone, and shoppers paying merchants like Flipkart or Myntra. A score is an investigation lead, not proof that someone committed a crime.
